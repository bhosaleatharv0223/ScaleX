over stock-looking graphics where possible. Avoid generic "startup SaaS" 
gradients/blobs — this audience is real estate developers, not consumers.

After Figma generates the first pass

Things to manually check/fix — AI design tools are decent at layout but often get these wrong:


Contrast: green-on-white CTA buttons sometimes fail accessibility contrast — check against WCAG AA before finalizing
Real vs placeholder copy: swap any Lorem Ipsum or generic AI-written copy with your actual pillar descriptions from the build brief
Icon consistency: AI tools often mix icon styles across sections — pick one icon set (e.g. Lucide, Phosphor) and enforce it manually
Image cropping: your uploaded photography may get stretched or oddly cropped by the AI — recrop manually to match your brand's existing photography style
